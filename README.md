# RUET Connect

> A centralized campus community platform designed exclusively for students of Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET).

**RUET Connect** is a student-focused website that brings essential campus resources and community services into one place. It provides RUET students with a convenient platform to access academic resources, discover university clubs, connect with fellow students, find lost items, donate blood, and buy or sell second-hand items within the campus community.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔐 Student Authentication
- Secure student registration and login
- RUET student account-based access
- User profile management
- Student academic and personal information
- Role-based user information

### 👤 Student Profiles
- Personal student profile
- Student ID and department information
- Hall and academic details
- Blood group information
- Contact information
- View other students' profiles

### 📚 Notes Vault
A centralized space for sharing and accessing academic resources.

- Upload and share course notes
- Browse notes by department
- Filter by semester
- Search by subject/course code
- Search by teacher or topic
- Download shared academic resources
- Support for different academic resources and study materials

### 🔎 Lost & Found
A dedicated campus lost-and-found system.

- Post lost or found items
- Add relevant item information
- Search and browse posts
- Contact the relevant student
- Remove resolved posts
- Display useful information about the person who posted an item

### 🩸 Blood Donation
A student-oriented blood donor directory.

- Search for blood donors
- Filter donors by blood group
- Access relevant donor information
- Help students quickly find potential donors within the RUET community

### 🛒 Sell & Buy Marketplace
A campus marketplace for second-hand items.

Students can post and discover:

- Books and academic materials
- Electronics and gadgets
- Bicycles and transport items
- Hall and room essentials
- Other useful items

Additional functionality includes:

- Product search
- Category filtering
- Price sorting
- Newest-first sorting
- Product posting
- Seller information

### 🏫 RUET Clubs Hub
Explore student clubs and organizations at RUET.

- Browse available clubs
- Club-specific information
- Club logos and visual identity
- Dedicated club detail pages
- Organized club directory
